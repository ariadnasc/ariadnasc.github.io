- [CDT in Natural Language Processing](https://informatics.ed.ac.uk/ukri-cdt-in-natural-language-processing), Cohort 2023
- Member, [Centre for Speech Technology Research](https://www.cstr.ed.ac.uk/)
- Activity Coordinator and Member, [Young IT Girls](https://www.instagram.com/youngitgirls_/)

I am working towards a PhD, supervised by [Prof. Simon King](https://homepages.inf.ed.ac.uk/simonk/) and [Dr. Korin Richmond](https://homepages.inf.ed.ac.uk/korin/sitenew/index.html), at the University of Edinburgh. My main PhD topic focuses on building personalised Text-to-Speech models for speakers with neurodegenerative conditions.

I have been in the Text-to-Speech field for almost 10 years, first by completing the MSc in Speech and Language Processing (207-2018) and then working at Amazon Alexa's Text-to-Speech team for 5 years (2018-2023).
